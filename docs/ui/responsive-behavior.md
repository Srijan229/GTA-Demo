# Responsive behavior

- At 375px, navigation uses a drawer, forms are single-column, actions stay near their content, and collections use accessible cards when a table cannot remain usable.
- At 768px, compact two-column layouts are allowed when labels and controls remain readable.
- At 1280px, the persistent sidebar and full operational tables are available.
- At 1440px and wider, content uses bounded widths and intentional whitespace.
- Dialogs never exceed the viewport; uploads remain keyboard and touch accessible; no page may introduce horizontal document overflow.
